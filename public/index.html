About Lubica

I’ve worked in English-speaking environments since graduating — including three years abroad for an American company and time in South Africa. Back in Slovakia I started tutoring privately in 2004, and I’ve been self-employed with a trade licence since 2009.

I hold a C2 diploma in English translation from the State Language School in Bratislava. My work combines company lessons tailored to employees’ needs, complex language audits, professional English–Slovak translation, and one-to-one or small-group tutoring. I also teach for language schools.
